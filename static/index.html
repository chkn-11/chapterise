Transcription backlog

How the queue works

Queue saved projects or upload several audiobooks. One book runs at a time, even with the browser closed. Keep the server running. You can review chapters while the queue works.

Scheduling and pausing

Settings and start time are saved separately for each task. Pause stops the current task at the next safe point; the unfinished chunk is repeated on resume. Removing a task keeps its uploaded book and saved project.

Pause detection

Pause settings

A threshold nearer zero detects quieter speech as silence. Spacing selects likely breaks; shorter gaps remain available to review.

Match chapters from the EPUB

Upload a matching EPUB to locate its chapters in the audiobook.

How matching works

The assistant searches chapter-opening phrases, widens its search for adapted passages, and checks chapter order. A later passage is a navigation clue, not a confirmed chapter boundary. Confidence labels describe evidence, not a probability.

Review your chapters

Review controls and shortcuts

Check the markers to include. Slide each marker up to 15 seconds before or after its original position, then Preview to listen. Use the arrow keys for millisecond adjustments, or type a start time as HH:MM:SS.mmm or seconds. Sliders stop at the audio boundaries; the first chapter stays at zero. Scanning again replaces pause suggestions; saved manual and EPUB markers are kept.

Add a missing chapter

How this works

Seek with the audio player, then use its playback position, or enter an exact time. Use “Insert after…” beside a marker to start looking in the gap after it.

New markers are inserted in time order and can be adjusted with the same ±15-second sliders.

Removing and restoring markers

Remove deletes a marker from this review and merges its audio into the preceding chapter on export. Undo removal restores the last deleted marker. The opening marker stays at zero.
